BudgetPix's compute-adaptive tokenization, adapted to image-to-3D generation (Idol, Idol-Turbo).

Overview

Budget3D fine-tunes Idol and Idol-Turbo, which turn one image into 3D Gaussian splats, so that the number of tokens is a setting you choose at inference. The regions where the latent varies least, empty space first, are merged into coarser tokens, and the full-resolution tokens go where it varies most.
